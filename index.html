About

A clinical beginning, now turning toward rigorous ML.

I began in clinical medicine, trained as a physician, and moved into radiology research, where I worked on CT-derived imaging biomarkers, body composition analysis, pancreatic disease, liver cancer, GI bleeding CTA, and outcomes-focused clinical imaging studies.

From November 2024 to May 2026, I was a Postdoctoral Scholar in Radiology at the University of Washington, Seattle. I am now beginning Columbia Engineering’s CS@CU MS Bridge / MSCS pathway, where I plan to build a rigorous foundation in computer science and machine learning.

I am especially interested in thesis-driven research that connects robust ML methods with clinically meaningful AI systems: models that can reason across imaging, text, structured clinical data, and longitudinal patient trajectories.

Research Interests

Questions I keep returning to.

The methods matter, but so does the clinical surface they meet: the messiness of real data, the cost of uncertainty, and the need for systems that make reasoning more faithful rather than merely faster.

Multimodal medical AI

Models that connect imaging, text, structured data, pathology, and outcomes.

Computer vision for radiology

Image representations that stay close to anatomy, disease, and clinical use.

Robust and uncertainty-aware ML

Methods that know when to be cautious and how to expose uncertainty.

Causality and missingness

Clinical datasets are incomplete by design; inference must respect that.

Imaging biomarkers and body composition

CT-derived measurements of muscle, adipose tissue, frailty, and risk.

Clinical reasoning with imaging + text

Learning from reports, images, and the interpretive logic that connects them.

Longitudinal outcomes modeling

Patient trajectories, response over time, and clinically meaningful endpoints.

Human-centered diagnostic AI

Systems built around clinicians, patients, ambiguity, and accountable care.

Selected Projects

Clinical problems as a proving ground for better methods.

A

CT Body Composition & Surgical Outcomes

CT-based skeletal muscle and adipose tissue analysis using segmentation tools to study frailty, surgical risk, and longitudinal outcomes in abdominal surgery and pancreatic disease.

B

HCC / Y90 / Liver Transplant Imaging Biomarkers

Analysis of patients undergoing Y90 treatment and liver transplant, including lesion response, downsizing, MELD score changes, longitudinal CT scans, and long-term outcomes.

C

GI Bleed CTA Reader Study

Reader-study and outcomes analysis of CTA for gastrointestinal bleeding, including active extravasation, non-active extravasation, dual-energy iodine maps, diagnostic confidence, and inter-reader agreement.

D

Multimodal Clinical AI Direction

Future-facing work focused on models that combine imaging, reports, clinical variables, pathology, and longitudinal outcomes to support diagnostic reasoning and patient-specific decision-making.
