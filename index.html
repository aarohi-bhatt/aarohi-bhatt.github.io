Medicine + Machine Learning

I study how clinical data, imaging, and human reasoning can become safer AI systems.

I am an MBBS-trained physician from India and a former Postdoctoral Scholar in Radiology at the University of Washington (Nov 2024 - May 2026). I am now beginning Columbia Engineering's CS@CU MS Bridge / MSCS pathway.

My long-term goal is to build clinically useful AI that can reason across images, reports, structured data, pathology, and patient trajectories while staying honest about uncertainty and missingness.

Research Interests

Multimodal medical AI

Models that connect imaging, text, structured data, pathology, and outcomes.

Human-centered diagnostic AI

Systems built around clinicians, patients, ambiguity, and accountable care.

Radiology vision + clinical reasoning

Image representations that stay close to anatomy, disease, reports, and interpretive logic.

Longitudinal, causal, missingness-aware ML

Methods for patient trajectories where absent data may reflect access, selection, or inequity.

Selected Work

HCC / Y90 / Transplant

Clinical AI direction

Incomplete multimodal clinical data

Future work on robust models that learn from messy real-world EHR, imaging, notes, genomics, and follow-up data.
